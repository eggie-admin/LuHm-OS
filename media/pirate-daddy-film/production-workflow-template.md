## Production handoff

1. Professor selects story and emotional payoff; identify exact screenplay/seal.
2. Yume drafts shot list, blocking, color script, character turns, and candidate sprite masks; Lum coordinates; Urd checks reference and rights contamination; Skuld checks FFmpeg/render/format compatibility; Belldandy logs choices and receipts.
3. Ingest only approved private Drive sources to a bounded private remote workspace. Sumi records hashes, artist/rights/visibility and per-shot lineage. Do not copy private files into public GitHub.
4. Assemble a **silent** 1080x1920 @ 30fps animatic through the remote GitHub Garage Lab and the audited AI-FFmpeg candidate workflow. Audio is deferred and should not be invented.
5. Verify montage playback, alpha, aspect ratio, color/pixel fidelity, screenshot receipt, final hash and continuity. Upload reviewed binary candidate to owner-controlled Drive storage.
6. Professor reviews. Only explicit approval permits promotion, public final render or Facebook publication.

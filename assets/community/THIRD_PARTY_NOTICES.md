## External packs not mirrored here

`community-assets.json` records additional vetted packs from Kenney, Poly Haven, and Quaternius. Their raw binary packs are intentionally not mirrored until an exact asset selection, checksum, and current-license receipt exist.

LuHm OS doctrine remains: community assets are ingredients, never runtime authority. No asset package may introduce executable plugins, shell bridges, secrets, remote control, or autonomous publishing.

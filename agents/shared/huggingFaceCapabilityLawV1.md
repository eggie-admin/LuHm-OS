# Hugging Face Capability Law v1

Canonical provider registry: `doctrine/providerOrchestraV1.json`.

Hugging Face is a bounded specialist capability lane behind Lum. It is not a boss, canon authority, source-truth authority, or GREEN authority.

## Shared capability map

- `modelDiscovery` — find candidate models for a bounded task.
- `spaceDiscovery` — discover useful Spaces/MCP demos for private evaluation.
- `paperResearch` — current research support.
- `datasetResearch` — dataset discovery and metadata only unless a task explicitly approves use.
- `backgroundRemoval` — masks/alpha prep.
- `imageSegmentation` — subject/region masks and decomposition.
- `depthMap` — depth/parallax assist and 3D blocking.
- `poseStructure` — pose/structure reference assistance.
- `imageTo3dResearch` — private prototype geometry research.
- `batchAssetProcessing` — explicit bounded Jobs workloads.
- `futureInferenceEndpoint` — stable serving only after separate cost/security review.

## Orchestra routing

- **Lum** signs and dispatches every provider job.
- **Urd** may sponsor diagnostic/anomaly/visual-pathology jobs.
- **Belldandy** may sponsor metadata normalization, receipt indexing, and comparison jobs.
- **Skuld** may sponsor model/Space/paper/dataset/license/compatibility research.
- **Yume** may sponsor art-production assist jobs such as masking, depth, pose, segmentation and prototype geometry.
- **Sumi** verifies provider/model identity, license/provenance, hashes and retained output identity.
- **Momo** may use HF research results only for the bounded question Lum delegated.
- **Shiori** may challenge a provider-derived claim or candidate but does not run provider work by default.
- **Kiri** may resolve provider evidence references into the active context packet.

Workers sponsor. Lum dispatches. Provider returns candidate evidence or candidate assets. Professor remains final authority.

## Cost and privacy

Temporary CPU/GPU Jobs require an explicit bounded task. Do not keep idle compute running. Protected references require explicit approval before provider upload. Public generative art publication remains prohibited by current LuHm art doctrine.

Provider output is `observed`, never automatically `green`, `approvedArt`, or `runtimeImportProven`.

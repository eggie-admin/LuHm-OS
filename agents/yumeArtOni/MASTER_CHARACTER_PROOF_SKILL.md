A master proof must demonstrate one recognizable character across multiple contexts, not several unrelated redesigns. Include a core turnaround/hero plus representative wardrobe, expression, sprite/pet and runtime-oriented variants when useful.

KAI9000, Cathedral, Coffee House, Riverwalk, workshop, vendor council, Tourniquet, Witching Hour and DeepDungeon appearances are contexts, not separate characters.

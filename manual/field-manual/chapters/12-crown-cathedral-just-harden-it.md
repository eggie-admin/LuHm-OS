## Professor Lecture

The final hardening pattern is simple: Test -> Audit -> Seal -> Document -> Promote.

Hardening means reducing ambiguity, unsupported state, hidden authority, fragile dependencies, and unreproducible behavior. It does not mean declaring perfection.

The current documentation workflow itself is GREEN. The broader source truth remains AMBER until the unresolved runtime/device/release gates are satisfied.

A finished field manual can be crowned as documentation without pretending the entire product is release-ready.

## Lum Tech Demo / Field Notes

- TEST: prove the claim.
- AUDIT: validate assumptions, security, architecture, and evidence.
- SEAL: record exact verified state and hashes.
- DOCUMENT: teach the why, not only the button.
- PROMOTE: human Crown advances only the scope that is proved.

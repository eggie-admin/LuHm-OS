## Professor Lecture

Human-centered engineering assumes operators get tired, distracted, sick, interrupted, or overloaded. The workflow should remain recoverable anyway.

Use clear names. Keep commands reproducible. Preserve receipts. Make state visible. Prefer reversible steps. Do not depend on perfect memory.

Humor is allowed. The lesson underneath is operational resilience.

## Lum Tech Demo / Field Notes

- Checkpoint before mutation.
- One bounded action at a time.
- Readable logs and friendly names.
- Stop/resume points that preserve state.
- No irreversible cleanup before the replacement is verified.

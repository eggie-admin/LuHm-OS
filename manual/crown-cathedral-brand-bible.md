## Zero-rebrand principle

Every new interface, character form, device, trailer, app package, or business channel **inherits from a small stable master identity**. A future hardware driver or widget cannot rename the publisher, studio, game universe, icon family, or authorship. Each brand alteration gets a semantic version, alias migration, tested rendering derivatives, and specific human approval. Preserve historical titles and spellings rather than rewriting past releases.

## Release versus design freeze

**Can approve now:** hierarchy, naming rules, derived-art taxonomy, protected/public routing, UI family taxonomy, and source-only tests.  
**Cannot responsibly certify yet:** every approved logo export, every original character mesh, legally licensed music, final video render, live ChatGPT widget UI, physical Samsung game install or public Instagram cleanup.

Build a **brand seal receipt** before saying Crown: source commit, owner scope, reviewed logo/art hash, release lane, rights, public output, and rollback. The design system may become stable by review without granting protected-main merge, account changes or publication.

**State:** Professor-directed brand freeze staged; final Crown and external publishing separately gated. No private Drive identifiers, raw reference photos, OAuth tokens or original unpublished scripts belong in public Git.

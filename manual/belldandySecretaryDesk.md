**Status: proposed editorial workflow, not an executing autonomous agent.** Lum supervises the in-chat roles. The Professor retains Crown and publication authority.

## Four desks

**Brand desk:** consistent LuHm OS, Project Premonition (legacy Project Hydra), Eggie Bagelface and PIRATE DADDIES FILM identities; collect marks, variants, credits, rights and review status.

**Field manual desk:** chapter routes, source links, version labels, print-edition references, clear examples and handoff notes.

**Writer's desk:** original dictated words stay intact; drafts, grammar suggestions, approved copies and story canon remain distinct.

**Receipt desk:** baseline SHA, changed paths, exact evidence, what was tested, gaps, rollback and next step.

## Supervised secretary pass

1. Resolve current main, [source truth](../doctrine/currentSourceTruthV3.json), relevant human writing, and repository/Drive boundaries.
2. Search for a canonical entry before creating a duplicate. Link existing source before copying bytes.
3. Classify every record as Observed, Historical, Proposed, Unverified or Blocked, and preserve authorship.
4. Prepare small source changes with readable diffs; run available link, syntax and scope checks.
5. Seal an exact-source receipt. Merge or publish only within authorized boundaries, never by self-assessed GREEN.
6. Escalate conflicts, rights gaps, unknown private/public scope and release blockers to Professor.

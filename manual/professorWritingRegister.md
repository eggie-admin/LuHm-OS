# Professor Original-Writing Register

**Status:** public-safe discovery register; unpublished source text remains private until selected and approved.

## Workflow states

**RawDictation:** verbatim captured writing/audio, never overwritten. **Draft:** proposed grammatical or editorial derivative. **InReview:** waiting for Professor's approval. **Approved:** exact accepted reading copy, date and project scope. **Superseded:** previous version preserved. **Blocked:** unknown author, privacy, rights or conflicting canon.

Maintain original source, credit, original hash, revision, changes, editor, canon scope, approval and public export reference for each approved transfer. Do not invent missing hashes or approval records.

## Copyedit protocol

1. Preserve the original exact text, timestamps and attribution.
2. Prepare a clearly labeled corrected draft that keeps the Professor's voice.
3. Check continuity and separate creative changes from spelling changes.
4. Require Professor to choose the accepted revision and permitted audience.
5. Keep original and superseded versions; publish only the approved derivative and safe provenance.

The October 20 Pirate Daddies Film production law specifically says raw dictation and original assets must remain intact and that approved project notes control project-specific canon. It does not make those private projects part of the LuHm OS public software release.

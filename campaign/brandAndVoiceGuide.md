## Professor's writing is the product voice

Write for the interested person, not a legal department. Keep short steps, a concrete test and a visible next action. A joke may introduce a lesson, then explain the exact rule. Preserve intentional word choice, humor, first-person perspective, directness and emotion.

Never silently reassign human authorship to AI. Keep raw dictation unchanged. Grammar polish and rewrites are **distinct drafts** until the Professor selects an exact approved reading copy. Do not strip third-party creator credits. Never treat unpublished personal or project scripts as public just because they were found in Drive.

The already-public [field manual](../manual/LUHM_OS_PROJECT_HYDRA_FIELD_MANUAL.md) and [campaign writing](github-project-narrative.md) are useful voice references. New public posts still require exact approval.

## Storage and publication

GitHub is the canonical source/metadata/receipt lane. Owner-controlled Drive remains the durable home for large illustrated manuals, editable art, private donor footage, raw narration and unpublished text. Do not commit private Drive IDs, transcripts, personal information, unlicensed images, secrets or signing keys.

Source -> authorship -> rights -> accurate editing -> hashes/receipts -> accessibility -> Professor's exact-art/publication approval -> distribution. Repository merge alone does not satisfy a finished public release.

## Threads / short post

I have spent roughly three decades around web and open-source development. Lately I have been building LuHm OS from a Samsung phone while walking around and talking to AI systems.

The interesting part is not “AI writes code.”

The interesting part is how far conversational/no-code operation can go **without giving up source identity, deterministic tests, receipts, or human authority.**

That is the experiment.

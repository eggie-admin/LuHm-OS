Lum & the Goddesses 💜

Try our independent-studio workflow. Expand a scenario below to discover how Lum coordinates the work, and which specialist owns each proof. No login or downloads required.

Read-only preview: These are documented responsibilities, not live AI agents or a connected ChatGPT plugin. Nothing here activates an agent, gathers personal data, or runs model inference.

🎨 Create a character, animation, or video

Lum: coordinates creative intent. Yume: owns the Blender/Godot/FFmpeg art candidate. Belldandy: tracks naming, versions and canon. Urd: challenges provenance or broken proofs. Review rights before public release.

🛠️ Debug code, Godot, or a plugin

Lum: keeps one task and source identity. Skuld: researches versions, compatibility and infrastructure. Urd: identifies the smallest proving test. Belldandy: preserves a reproducible issue and rollback reference.

🧪 Audit a failure or security concern

Lum: prioritizes the risk. Urd: separates observed errors from guesses. Skuld: checks technical causes and approved references. Belldandy: records evidence without changing permissions. No live infrastructure is altered by this preview.

🚀 Prepare a tester or public release

Lum: coordinates the release candidate. Belldandy: reconciles namespace, receipts and source. Skuld: verifies technical compatibility. Urd: reviews uncertain evidence. The human creator authorizes any consequential publication.
